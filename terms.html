What Daysafe does

Daysafe counts the days you spend in each country, using your phone's location and what you enter yourself, and compares them with the rules you choose, such as Schengen 90/180 or a 183-day tax rule. It shows how each rule is counted.

What Daysafe doesn't do

Daysafe is not tax, immigration or legal advice. Residency and visa decisions can depend on things the app can't see, such as your home, family, income, passport and visa type, and on rules that change. A phone can miss a day or place you in the wrong country, for example near a border or when location is switched off. Check your days, fix any that are wrong, and confirm important decisions with an official source or a professional. You are responsible for the decisions you make.
